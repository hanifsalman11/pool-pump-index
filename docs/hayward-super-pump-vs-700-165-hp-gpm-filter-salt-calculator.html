Frequently Asked Questions (Hayward Super Pump VS 700 1.65 HP)

How many GPM do I need for Hayward Super Pump VS 700 1.65 HP (16,000 gallons)?

To filter 16,000 gallons once every 8 hours (480 minutes), your pump must deliver 33 Gallons Per Minute (1,980 GPH). For a 6-hour commercial/summer turnover, increase flow to 44 GPM.

What size saltwater chlorinator cell do I need for 16,000 gallons?

Always size your salt cell at 1.5x to 2x your actual water volume. For 16,000 gallons, install a 40,000-gallon rated salt cell (such as a T-Cell-15 or IC40) along with 11 bags (40 lbs each) of pool salt to reach 3,200 ppm.

How much electricity does a variable-speed pump save on a 16,000-gallon pool?

By affinity laws, cutting pump RPM in half reduces power consumption to one-eighth (12.5%). Running a 1.65 HP variable-speed pump at low speed draws only 180W–280W compared to 1,800W+ on a single-speed pump, saving $650 to $1,100 per year in utility bills.
